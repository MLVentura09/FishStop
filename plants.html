Low-tech tanks do not use injected CO2 gas. They grow slowly under dim or medium light and survive on natural fish waste and basic nutrients.

  • -Java Fern
  • -Anubias
  • -Jungle Vallisneria
  • -Marimo Moss Ball
  • -Cryptocoryne

High-tech aquarium plants require quality lighting, pressurized carbon dioxide (CO2), and nutrient-rich fertilizers to get rapid growth and vibrant colors.

  • -Dwarf Baby Tears (Hemianthus callitrichoides)
  • -Rotala Macrandra
  • -Hairgrass (Eleocharis acicularis)
  • -Rotala H’ra (Rotala Gia Lai)
  • -Staurogyne sp. Brown
