Community fish are small, gentle species that live harmoniously with other peaceful kinds of fish. They thrive in groups and fit well into standard home aquariums, including planted tanks.

  • -Betta Fish
  • -Gold Fish
  • -Mollies
  • -Guppies
  • -Tetra

Monster fish are large, fast-growing aquatic species that often need tanks holding 100 to 500 gallons or more. They are usually predatory, highly active, and require specialized, heavy-duty filtration systems.

  • -Oscar
  • -Redtail
  • -Bichirs
  • -Pacu
  • -Pleco
